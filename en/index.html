    How to search

    • In English: heart, autumn, to see
    • By an Old Hangul form: ᄆᆞᅀᆞᆷ, ᄀᆞᅀᆞᆯ, ᄒᆞᆰ
    • Syllable division does not matter: 사ᄅᆞᆷ also finds 사ᄅᆞᄆᆞᆯ・사ᄅᆞᆷᄆᆞᆯ, and 놀애 finds 노래
    • By the modern headword: 가을, 마음
    • In Yale romanization: mozom, kozol (Latin letters are matched against both English and Yale)

    With “Allow each century’s spelling variation”, letters and spellings that had already merged in the century of a form are treated as the same: for forms from the 17th century on, ㅿ is ignored and ㅴ・ㅺ・ㄲ are not distinguished; from the 18th century on, ㆍ and ㅏ in the first syllable, and 디 and 지, are not distinguished (rules after Lee & Ramsey 2011, ch. 6). Untick periods to search only the forms of those centuries.
